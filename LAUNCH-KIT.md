I built Monarch Art Vault because I had thousands of art photos in chaos folders—no titles, tons of duplicates, no idea what was print-ready.

What it does:
- Archives originals (never deletes)
- Deduplicates via SHA-256 + perceptual hashing
- Generates metadata (titles, descriptions, tags) marked as AI suggestions
- Calculates print sizes at 300 DPI
- Exports to CSV/Excel/Google Sheets
- Drafts marketplace listings (Etsy, Shopify, Printful)

Key design decisions:
- Human approval required for any destructive or external action
- AI suggestions clearly labeled with confidence scores
- Source provenance tracked for every field
- Works with any AI agent (Claude, GPT-4, Hermes, LangChain)

It's a "skill" (structured prompt + schemas + scripts) rather than a full app, so it plugs into whatever AI workflow you're already using.

MIT licensed: https://github.com/diamitani/monarch-art-vault

Feedback welcome, especially on the metadata schema and approval model.
